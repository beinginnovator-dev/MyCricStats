SPORTMONKS CRICKET API 2.0 — IMPLEMENTATION NOTES
=================================================
Base URL: https://cricket.sportmonks.com/api/v2.0/

Fixture endpoint documented includes used by MyCricStats:
- localteam, visitorteam
- runs and runs.team (innings totals and team mapping)
- batting and batting.batsman (batter scorecard and player relation)
- bowling and bowling.bowler (bowling figures and player relation)
- lineup (playing XI and lineup/player fields)
- venue (ground details)

Ball-by-ball is requested separately to keep the fixture call small:
- balls
- balls.batsman
- balls.bowler
- balls.score
- balls.batsmanout
- balls.catchstump
- balls.runoutby

Data mapping:
- Fixture localteam_id / visitorteam_id define the two sides.
- runs.team_id maps each innings score to a team.
- runs.inning corresponds to batting/bowling scoreboard key (S1/S2/S3/S4).
- batting.score, batting.ball, batting.four_x, batting.six_x provide batter figures.
- bowling.overs, bowling.medians, bowling.runs, bowling.wickets provide bowling figures.
- balls.ball, balls.batsman_id, balls.bowler_id, balls.score.runs, balls.score.name, balls.score.is_wicket provide delivery detail.
- lineup carries the playing XI, player names and team_id; lineup stats can be displayed where returned.

Do not request `lineup.player` on the fixture endpoint: the user's endpoint explicitly rejected it.
Do not merge the same over number across separate innings. Preserve inning keys when grouping commentary.
Do not infer a team's score from whether it appears first or second in an array.
